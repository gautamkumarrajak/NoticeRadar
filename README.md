# NoticeRadar
An unofficial university notice monitoring and alert system.

## Problem

Important university and college notices are often published on official
websites without personalized alerts. Students may depend on manually
checking websites, class groups, forwarded messages, or classmates to learn
about important notices.

## Solution

NoticeRadar automatically monitors public official notice sources, collects
new notices, organizes them by category and academic relevance, and provides
students with a centralized feed and alerts.

Every notice links back to its original official source.
